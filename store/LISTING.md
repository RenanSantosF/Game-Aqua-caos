# Textos da ficha da Play Store

Copie e cole na Play Console → **Presença na loja → Página principal da
loja**. Adicione os idiomas em **Gerenciar traduções**.

Limites: título com até 30 caracteres, descrição curta com até 80 e
descrição completa com até 4000.

Imagens de cada idioma ficam na pasta dele:

- `store/pt-BR`, `store/en-US` e `store/es-419`;
- banner: `feature_graphic_1024x500.png`;
- capturas: `screenshot_1..8.png`, 1920×1080;
- ícone (igual para todos): `store/icon_512.png`.

## English (United States): en-US

**Title:** Aqua Caos: Splash Challenge

**Short description:**
Splashy obstacle races! Jump, dive and beat your rivals at the water park.

**Full description:**

Welcome to the most chaotic water park ever! 🌊

In **Aqua Caos: Splash Challenge** you race against rivals through
obstacle courses over the water. Jump, dive, dodge giant hammers and
finish first without falling into the pool!

🏁 **FUN-PACKED RACES**
- Swinging hammers, spinning bars, rolling logs, wind fans and bouncy
  floats.
- Water slides, curves, drops and jump ramps.
- Tap JUMP to jump, then tap again in the air to dive.

🗺️ **ENDLESS LEVELS**
- An adventure map with endless levels.
- Every level is a different course, and new obstacles show up as you
  progress.
- Earn up to 3 stars on each level and beat your best times.

🎨 **FUN SKINS**
- Froggy, Penguin, Pirate, Unicorn, Shark, Bubble Bot and many more.
- Collect coins from races and the daily gift.

🌴 **COLORFUL WORLDS**
- Tropical Park, Sunset Bay, Neon Lagoon at night, Candy Island and Coral
  Reef.

🎵 Cheerful music and simple controls, made to play with one hand.

Play offline: your progress is saved on your device.
